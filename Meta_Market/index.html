Terms & Conditions

1. Payment Terms

Cash on Delivery (COD) Only: All orders are strictly Cash on Delivery. You must pay the full order amount in cash directly to the delivery driver upon receiving your groceries. Please keep the exact amount ready for a smooth transaction.

2. Order Confirmation

By placing an order, you agree to provide accurate delivery information and be available at the specified address during the delivery window. An order confirmation will be sent to your email with all order details.

3. Delivery

We strive to deliver fresh groceries within the estimated time frame. Delivery times may vary based on your location and order volume. The delivery driver will contact you upon arrival.

4. Product Quality

We ensure all products are fresh and of the highest quality. If you receive damaged or unsatisfactory products, please inform the delivery driver immediately before accepting the delivery.

5. Cancellation Policy

Orders can be cancelled within 30 minutes of placement. Once the order is being prepared or out for delivery, cancellation will not be possible.

6. Pricing

All prices are listed in Indian Rupees (₹) and are inclusive of applicable taxes. Prices are subject to change without prior notice. The final amount will be calculated at the time of order placement.

⚠️ Important Reminder

You must pay the delivery driver in cash when your order arrives. No online payment is accepted at this time. Please have the exact amount or sufficient cash ready.
